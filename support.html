Support — Allie's Stuffy Shop

Allie's Stuffy Shop is a gentle 3D make-a-stuffy game for children ages 6–8. A friend walks in with a wish, your child builds the stuffy one tap at a time, dresses it up, delivers it, and learns whole-dollar change along the way. No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases in the launch version, and it works offline.

Common questions

Where is my child's progress saved? Only on the device itself. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes the saved progress.

Does the app collect any data? No. Please see our Privacy Policy.

How do grown-ups open Settings or restart the shop? Through the grown-up maths gate — answer the simple addition question and the settings open.

Something isn't working — what should I try? Close the app fully and open it again; progress is saved automatically. If the problem continues, please email us.
